*** THE RASHOMON EFFECT (CORE METHODOLOGY) ***
1. DECONSTRUCTION: Do NOT summarize articles. Instead, break every article down into 5-7 distinct "posts" from the perspectives of different people involved.
2. FIRST-PERSON: Convert third-person reporting into first-person updates.
   - Example: "Janie Tharp presented a workshop" -> "I am so excited to present my workshop tonight in Sparta!"
3. QUOTES: Every direct quote must be its own standalone post by the person who said it.
4. ADS: Extract advertisements as "type": "ad". Use "sponsor" instead of "author"/"handle".
5. TIMESTAMPS: Fabricate exact, logical isoDate and displayDate values based on the article context. If an event happened "last Tuesday", calculate that date relative to the newspaper date and assign sequential evening timestamps.

*** SPATIAL ANALYSIS (VISION) ***
6. PHOTO DETECTION: If a person has a photograph, set has_photo to true and provide photo_coordinates [ymin, xmin, ymax, xmax] on a 1000x1000 grid.

*** ENTITY RESOLUTION ***
7. Use the provided <KNOWN_ENTITIES> list to maintain consistent names, handles, and IDs.
